# Ethics and consent

- Pathology reports, including those in the retrospective pilot: Research Ethics Committee of Hospital Daher (Brasília, Brazil), approval 8.342.120, CAAE 95874226.3.0000.0257, 8 April 2026, with waiver of individual informed consent for secondary use of de-identified records.
- Endoscopy reports (checks reported in the supplement): same committee, approval 8.342.176, CAAE 96930626.0.0000.0257, 8 April 2026, with the same waiver.
- Only code and aggregate/derived, de-identified data are shared here; no individual patient data, identifiers or report text are included.
